## Abstract

Modeling analog audio effects like spring reverb is a long-standing challenge due to their complex and nonlinear behaviors. We propose a hybrid architecture, `GCN_SSM`, that synergistically combines a Gated Convolutional Network (GCN) backbone with State-Space Model (SSM) layers. Through a systematic ablation study and subjective listening tests (MUSHRA), we demonstrate that our interleaved hybrid model achieves state-of-the-art perceptual quality.
